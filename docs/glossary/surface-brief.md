# Surface brief

A surface brief is the strategy file for one route, page or artifact, stored under `.impeccable/surfaces/` in the user's project. It holds what belongs to that surface alone: its [mode](mode.md), the direction contract written before code, and the approved [comp](comp.md) path on a comp-led build. `impeccable context` loads the brief that matches the target it is given.

Not to be confused with [PRODUCT.md](product-md.md), which holds product truth for the whole project, or [DESIGN.md](design-md.md), which holds durable visual decisions. A rule that applies to every surface does not go in a brief.
